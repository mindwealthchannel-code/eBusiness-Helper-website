Why Shopify + SEO is Crucial

Many people think running Facebook or Google Ads is enough. Here is the harsh truth: Ads only work as long as you keep paying. The moment you stop your ad budget, your sales drop to zero.

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is different. Google's algorithm ranks websites based on relevance, user experience, and quality content. When you invest in SEO, your website appears organically when people search for your products. This builds a permanent online presence and long-term brand trust, both locally and internationally.
