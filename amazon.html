Amazon FBA vs FBM (What's the difference?)

Understanding how your products are delivered is crucial for your profit margins.

FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon)

You send your inventory to Amazon's warehouses. Amazon handles storage, packing, shipping, and customer service. It's faster and Prime-eligible, but comes with storage and fulfillment fees.

FBM (Fulfillment by Merchant)

You store the inventory yourself (or in a 3PL) and ship it directly to the customer when they order. You save on Amazon's storage fees but have to manage logistics and customer service yourself.
